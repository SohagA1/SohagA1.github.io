Featured Project

Digital Safe Drop System

HTML/CSS JavaScript Google Apps Script
  • The Problem: The Finance department struggled with illegible, handwritten paper logs for cash safe drops, causing reconciliation delays and security tracking gaps.
  • The Solution: I engineered a custom, mobile-responsive web application to replace the paper folder entirely.
  • Key Features: Designed a dual-PIN digital signature verification system to prevent fraud, instantly pushing timestamped entries to a live, reverse-chronological cloud database for the Finance team.
  • The Impact: Eliminated paper waste, digitized the audit trail, and modernized the organization's cash-handling SOP. Approved and adopted by the Operations Manager.
