Commercial Strategy & E-commerce

International Supply Chain & Retail Logistics

Alongside facility management, I actively research and map out international e-commerce ventures. This involves deep-dive analysis into supply chain sourcing, import compliance, and retail distribution metrics to build viable, scalable business models.

Global Sourcing & Supplier Relations

  • Product Identification: Researching high-demand, high-margin household goods—such as specialized laundry baskets and floor mats—for import into the UK market.
  • Supplier Communication: Establishing contact with international manufacturers, evaluating production capabilities, negotiating unit costs, and assessing quality assurance protocols.
  • Cost-Benefit Analysis: Calculating landing costs by factoring in production, shipping, and potential tariffs to ensure healthy profit margins before committing to inventory.

Import Compliance & Retail Logistics

  • Regulatory Navigation: Investigating UK import compliance standards, ensuring all sourced materials meet domestic safety and trading regulations prior to cross-border transit.
  • Dimensional Optimization: Analyzing standard retail dimensions and packaging footprints to maximize shipping container efficiency and minimize dead space during transit.
  • Distribution Strategy: Mapping out domestic fulfillment networks and storage logistics to seamlessly bridge the gap between international arrival and the end consumer.
